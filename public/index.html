About Owen

Hey there, I'm Owen.

I've been doing audio since 2005. It started as a fun gig, and it turned into a whole career.

The gist

Just a guy who likes good sound.

I started out in recording studios, spent some late nights in clubs, worked a stretch at theme parks, and these days I'm mostly doing corporate events. Every stop taught me something, usually the hard way.

What I like most is when everything just works: the mics are clear, the people on stage can relax, and nobody has to think about the audio at all.

When I'm not at a show, I'm probably recording one of my podcasts.
